Is this phone number a scammer?

A lookup won't read the caller's mind, but it catches a lot. Here's how to run an is this phone number a scammer lookup in under a minute.

  1. Look it up. Paste the number at the top of the page.
  2. Check the badge. Invalid numbers are almost always spoofed.
  3. Check the line type. VoIP and premium-rate numbers show up a lot in scam calls.
  4. Tap Scam check. It searches for spam and robocall reports about that exact number.

Hang up if you hear any of these

  • “This is the IRS” or “Social Security”, and you need to pay today
  • Payment by gift card, crypto or wire transfer
  • A request to read back a code that was just texted to you
  • A one-ring call from a country you've never dealt with
  • Threats of arrest or a frozen account

In the US you can report these to the FTC and add yourself to the Do Not Call Registry.
